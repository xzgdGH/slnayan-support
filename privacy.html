SLnayan Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 4, 2026 · App: SLnayan

Thank you for using SLnayan (the “App”). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App. Please read it carefully before use.

1. Information We Collect

SLnayan is a companion app that connects your phone to a hardware device for live video preview, photo capture, and video recording. In general:

  • We do not intentionally collect personal identity information (such as your name, ID number, contacts, or precise location) for analytics, marketing, or advertising.
  • To provide core features, the App may access device capabilities such as Bluetooth, local network / Wi‑Fi, and Photos (to save images and videos), subject to your permission.
  • Wi‑Fi SSID and password obtained via Bluetooth from the device are used only to help your phone join the device hotspot for streaming. This information is processed on your device and is not uploaded to our servers.

2. How Information Is Used

  • Bluetooth: to discover and connect to a device named SLnayan and read the information needed to join its Wi‑Fi hotspot.
  • Wi‑Fi / Local Network: to connect to the device hotspot and receive video stream data.
  • Photos: to save photos and videos that you capture in the App to the system Photos library and/or the App’s local album folder.

3. Storage and Sharing

  • Captured photos and videos are stored locally on your device (and in the system Photos library if you authorize saving).
  • We do not sell your personal information to third parties.
  • We do not disclose your personal information except where required by law or necessary to protect users or public safety.

4. Permissions

You can manage App permissions at any time in your device Settings. Denying a permission may disable related features, but will not affect unrelated features.

5. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. If you believe we may have inadvertently collected information from a child, please contact us via the Support page so we can delete it.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will be posted on this page and becomes effective when published.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the Support page.

SLnayan 隐私政策

更新日期：2026年8月4日 · 应用：SLnayan

感谢您使用 SLnayan（以下简称“本应用”）。本政策说明您在使用本应用时，相关信息如何被处理。请在使用前仔细阅读。

1. 我们收集哪些信息

本应用主要用于通过手机连接硬件设备，进行图传预览、拍照与录像。通常情况下：

  • 我们不会主动收集您的姓名、身份证号、通讯录、精确位置等个人身份信息，用于运营分析、营销或广告。
  • 为实现功能，本应用可能在您授权后访问：蓝牙、本地网络 / Wi‑Fi、相册（保存照片与视频）等系统能力。
  • 通过蓝牙从设备获取的 Wi‑Fi 名称与密码，仅用于帮助手机连接设备热点以建立图传，相关处理发生在您的设备本地，不会因此上传至我们的服务器。

2. 信息如何使用

  • 蓝牙：搜索并连接名称为 SLnayan 的设备，读取连接 Wi‑Fi 所需信息。
  • Wi‑Fi / 本地网络：连接设备热点并接收图传数据。
  • 相册：将您主动拍摄的照片 / 视频保存到系统相册和 / 或本应用本地相册目录。

3. 信息存储与共享

  • 拍照 / 录像文件默认保存在您的设备本地（如您授权，也会保存到系统相册）。
  • 我们不会向第三方出售您的个人信息。
  • 除法律法规要求，或为保护用户 / 公共安全所必需外，我们不会对外披露您的个人信息。

4. 权限说明

您可随时在系统设置中管理本应用权限。拒绝某项权限可能导致对应功能不可用，但不影响其他未依赖该权限的功能。

5. 儿童隐私

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童。若您认为我们可能无意中收集了儿童信息，请通过技术支持页面联系我们删除。

6. 政策更新

我们可能适时更新本政策。更新后将在本页面发布，并以本页面最新版本为准。

7. 联系我们

如对本政策有疑问，请通过 技术支持页面 联系我们。
