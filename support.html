Frequently Asked Questions

  • Cannot connect to the device? Please turn on Bluetooth and Wi‑Fi on your phone, power on the device, then tap Start in the App to search again.
  • Permission prompts for Bluetooth or Local Network? Please allow the required permissions for SLnayan in your system Settings.
  • Connected but no video image? Move closer to the device and retry, or restart the device and the App, then connect again.
  • Where are photos and videos saved? You can view them in the App’s Album. If authorized, they may also be saved to the system Photos library.
  • Wi‑Fi join prompt cancelled? The App needs to join the device hotspot to receive the video stream. Please tap Start again and allow the Wi‑Fi connection when prompted.

常见问题

  • 无法连接设备？请确认手机已开启蓝牙与 Wi‑Fi，设备已开机，然后在 App 中点击「开始」重新搜索。
  • 提示需要蓝牙 / 本地网络权限？请到系统设置中允许 SLnayan 使用相关权限。
  • 能连上但没有图像？请靠近设备重试，或重启设备与 App 后再连接。
  • 拍照 / 录像保存在哪里？可在 App「相册」中查看；若已授权，也会同步到系统相册。
  • 取消了加入 Wi‑Fi 的提示？App 需要加入设备热点才能接收图传，请再次点击「开始」，并在系统提示时允许连接。
